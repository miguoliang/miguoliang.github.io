和谐家庭 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年6月26日 · 最近更新：2026年7月9日

「和谐家庭」（以下简称「本 App」）是一款帮助家庭成员记录日常表现、互相点评的本地工具。我们重视您的隐私。

1. 我们收集哪些信息？

我们不收集、不上传、不出售您的个人数据。本 App 不需要注册账号，也不会将您的数据发送到我们的服务器。

以下信息仅保存在您的设备上，由您和家人自行管理：

  • 家庭成员昵称、头像 emoji、角色（家长/孩子）
  • 您为每位家人设置的评分标准
  • 每日「棒 / 加油」点评记录
  • 用于登录验证的 PIN 码（以加密哈希形式本地存储，不以明文保存）

2. 数据存储与删除

所有数据通过 Apple 设备本地存储（SwiftData）保存在本机。卸载本 App 将删除设备上的相关数据。您也可以在 App 内「设置」中删除家庭成员及其记录。

3. 网络与第三方

本 App 不联网，不使用第三方广告或统计 SDK，不与第三方共享数据。

4. 儿童与家庭使用

本 App 面向家庭场景，由家长管理设置与成员信息。我们不会主动收集儿童的个人信息，也不会要求儿童单独注册账号。

5. 政策变更

若本政策有更新，我们会在本页面修订生效日期。重大变更时，我们可能通过 App 更新说明告知您。

6. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：boymgl@qq.com

Harmonious Family Privacy Policy

Effective: June 26, 2026 · Last updated: July 9, 2026

“Harmonious Family” (the “App”) is a local tool that helps family members track daily performance and review each other. We take your privacy seriously.

1. What information do we collect?

We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data. The App does not require an account and does not send your data to our servers.

The following information is stored only on your device and managed by you and your family:

  • Family member nicknames, avatar emoji, and roles (parent / child)
  • Criteria you set for each family member
  • Daily “Great / Keep going” review records
  • PINs used for sign-in (stored locally as encrypted hashes, never in plain text)

2. Storage and deletion

All data is stored on-device via Apple’s local storage (SwiftData). Uninstalling the App removes related data from the device. You can also delete family members and their records in Settings.

3. Network and third parties

The App does not use the network, does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not share data with third parties.

4. Children and family use

The App is designed for family use, with parents managing settings and member information. We do not actively collect children’s personal information and do not require children to create separate accounts.

5. Policy changes

If this policy is updated, we will revise the date on this page. For material changes, we may also note them in App update release notes.

6. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact: boymgl@qq.com
